ABOUT TRASHIGANG DZONG

Trashigang Dzong is one of the important historical fortresses of Bhutan. It is located in Trashigang District in eastern Bhutan, overlooking the Drangme Chhu valley.

The dzong is known for its traditional Bhutanese architecture, religious importance and role in local administration. It represents the rich cultural identity and heritage of eastern Bhutan.


HISTORY OF TRASHIGANG DZONG

Trashigang Dzong was built in the 17th century under the leadership of Chögyal Minjur Tenpa. It became an important centre for administration and religion in eastern Bhutan.

Over the years, the dzong has remained an important part of the region's history and continues to represent Bhutan's living cultural traditions.


ARCHITECTURE AND CULTURE

Traditional Bhutanese design, whitewashed walls, wooden windows and religious spaces make the dzong an important example of Bhutanese architectural heritage.


WHY IS TRASHIGANG DZONG IMPORTANT?

  • It preserves Bhutanese history and heritage.
  • It serves as an important religious centre.
  • It reflects traditional Bhutanese architecture.
  • It represents the cultural identity of eastern Bhutan.
